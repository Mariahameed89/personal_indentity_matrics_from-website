Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Demo disclaimer. PipelineFlow is a fictional company and this site exists only to test a third-party visitor-identification pixel (Leadpipe). No products or services are actually sold here.

1. What this site does

With your consent, this page loads a third-party visitor-identification tool provided by VisiLead (visilead.co). When enabled, VisiLead attempts to identify the business/organization associated with a visit (for example, based on network information). We use this only to test whether the tool functions. Other tools (Leadpipe, Warmly.ai) may be tested the same way in future.

2. Consent comes first

No visitor-identification tool is loaded at all until you click Accept on the cookie banner — before that, your browser makes no request to VisiLead whatsoever. If you click Reject, nothing is loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key pf_consent.

VisiLead is additionally loaded in its manual consent mode, meaning it stores and tracks nothing until we explicitly call visilead.consent() after you accept. It also honours the browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) opt-out signal automatically. Using Reset consent in the footer calls visilead.revokeConsent(), which stops tracking and deletes your visitor ID from the browser.

3. What may be collected (when you accept)

  • Standard request metadata such as IP address and user agent (by VisiLead).
  • Pages viewed on this site and basic interaction events used for the test.
  • Any details you voluntarily type into the demo contact form.

The demo contact form does not send data to any server of ours — submissions are handled entirely in your browser to show a thank-you message.

4. Third-party processing

VisiLead processes visit data on its own infrastructure under its own policies. Review VisiLead's documentation and privacy terms at visilead.co for details on how identification works and how to exercise data rights. The same applies to any other identification tool we test.

5. Cookies & local storage

  • pf_consent — your Accept/Reject choice (first-party, localStorage).
  • A VisiLead visitor ID and any cookies its script sets — only after you accept. These are deleted when you use Reset consent.

6. Your choices

  • Click Reject to prevent the pixel from loading.
  • Use Reset consent (footer) to clear your choice and be asked again.
  • Use your browser or an ad/tracker blocker to block third-party scripts.

7. Contact

This is a demo project. For questions about the test, contact the site operator who deployed it. For questions about Leadpipe's processing, contact Leadpipe directly.
